## 1. Overview

Sales Register records all **customer sales transactions** (sales invoices) generated by the business. Each row in this table represents a single invoice line item. 

The register links:
- `customer_id` (Customer Master) to retrieve customer tier, registration details, and credit terms.
- `company_id` (Company Master) to capture selling legal entity context.
- `product_id` (Product Master) to read physical engineering constants (weight, length, HSN, and GST rate).
- `price_id` (Price History) to reference the standard standard selling rate on the invoice date.

This table acts as the origin of financial receivables (accounts receivable) and inventory reductions (stock dispatches).

---

## 2. Business Purpose

**Why this table exists**
- Captures raw outbound sales data. Inventory deductions are derived from these lines (Product + Quantity).
- Computes customer credit utilization and receivables aging profiles, driving cash collection triggers in the Cashbook (10).
- Calculates output tax liabilities for GST returns. State fields are cross-referenced to split the flat 18.00% GST into CGST+SGST (intra-state) or IGST (inter-state) reporting columns.
- Serves as the database for sales price variance and margin analysis: analyzing how much actual sales rates deviated from the category-level Steel Market Index (05) and standard Price History (06) to measure profitability.

**How it is used**
- Inventory: running balances subtract the pieces and weight from each sales line on its transaction date.
- Cashbook: collection receipts are scheduled against outstanding sales invoice totals based on invoice due dates and customer payment behavior tiers.
- Compliance reporting: CGST, SGST, and IGST totals are aggregated for monthly GSTR-1 filings.

## 4. Business Rules

1. **Physical Attribute Integrity**: Physical constants (`weight_per_meter`, piece `length` = 6.00m, and `gst` rate = 18.00%) must be read directly from Product Master via `product_id`. No transaction line is allowed to override or recompute these fields.
2. **Pricing Baseline**: Every sales line item must map to a valid `price_id` in Price History (06). The `standard_selling_rate_per_kg` represents the standard list price for that product code on the `sales_date`.
3. **Actual Rate Derivation (Customer-Type Correlated)**: The actual transaction rate `actual_selling_rate_per_kg` is calculated as standard rate plus negotiation variance:
   $$\text{actual\_selling\_rate\_per\_kg} = \text{standard\_selling\_rate\_per\_kg} \times \left(1 + \frac{\text{negotiation\_variance\_pct}}{100}\right)$$
   The `negotiation_variance_pct` represents volume discounts, customer size multipliers, or retail markups:
   - `Distributor` (wholesale): -4.00% to -2.00% (volume discounts)
   - `Retailer` (standard dealer): -1.50% to +1.00%
   - `Contractor` (project-linked): -2.50% to 0.00%
   - `Fabricator` (retail/cash): 0.00% to +3.00% (spot premiums)
4. **GST Tax Split Rules**: GST is charged at a flat 18.00%.
   - **Intra-state sales** (Customer State = Company State): CGST is `taxable_value * 9.00%`, SGST is `taxable_value * 9.00%`, and IGST is `0.00`.
   - **Inter-state sales** (Customer State $\neq$ Company State): IGST is `taxable_value * 18.00%`, and CGST/SGST are both `0.00`.
5. **Calculated Total Weight**: The total line weight is:
   $$\text{total\_weight\_kg} = \text{quantity\_pcs} \times \text{length} \times \text{weight\_per\_meter}$$
   The value is rounded to three decimal places.
6. **Invoice Grouping Integrity**: Multiple line items can share the same `sales_invoice_number`. Lines sharing the same invoice number must have identical invoice-level metadata: `sales_date`, `customer_id`, `company_id`, `credit_period_days`, `payment_due_date`, and `payment_status`.
7. **Inventory Validation Constraint**: A sales invoice line is only valid if the transaction quantity `quantity_pcs` is less than or equal to the available warehouse stock at the start of that day plus today's purchases:
   $$\text{quantity\_pcs} \le \text{opening\_qty\_pcs (from Inventory on date T)} + \text{purchased\_qty\_pcs (from Inventory on date T)}$$
   This prevents database updates that result in physical stockouts.
8. **Due Date Math**: The payment deadline is calculated as:
   $$\text{payment\_due\_date} = \text{sales\_date} + \text{credit\_period\_days}$$
9. **Timeline Alignment**: The `sales_date` must occur on or after the customer's `onboarding_date` and on or after the company's `opening_balance_date`.
10. **State of Status**: All transactions are generated with `payment_status = 'Unpaid'` and outstanding value equal to the full invoice value. (Settlements are simulated during Cashbook generation).

## 5. Validation Rules

| # | Rule | Reject condition |
|---|---|---|
| V1 | Unique transaction lines | Two rows share the same `sales_line_id` |
| V2 | Stock availability verification | Product's transaction `quantity_pcs` exceeds available stock in Inventory (08) for that day |
| V3 | GST classification check | If `customer.state = company.state` but `igst_amount != 0` or CGST/SGST are incorrect; or if states differ but CGST/SGST are not `0.00` |
| V4 | Weight calculation accuracy | `total_weight_kg` deviates >±0.1% from `quantity_pcs * length * weight_per_meter` |
| V5 | Taxable value calculation accuracy | `taxable_value` deviates >₹0.05 from `total_weight_kg * actual_selling_rate_per_kg` |
| V6 | GST amount calculation accuracy | CGST/SGST/IGST amounts deviate >₹0.05 from standard 9.00% / 18.00% taxable value math |
| V7 | Due date validation | `payment_due_date` does not equal `sales_date + credit_period_days` |
| V8 | Date chronology validation | `sales_date` is before the customer's onboarding date or the company's opening balance date |
| V9 | Invoice coherence validation | Rows sharing a `sales_invoice_number` have mismatching `sales_date`, `customer_id`, `payment_due_date`, or `payment_status` |
| V10 | Pricing lookup validation | `standard_selling_rate_per_kg` does not match the active price in Price History for that product on that date |

## 7. Generation Rules

1. **Volume**: Generate transactions representing a mid-sized steel pipe distributor:
   - ~800 to 1,200 total sales invoices per FY (averaging 15–25 invoices per week, double the purchase volume reflecting smaller average order sizes).
   - Randomly sample 1 to 4 line items per invoice, yielding ~1,500 to 3,500 line item rows in the database per FY.
2. **Temporal Distribution**: Distribute sales across the financial year with a seasonal drop during the monsoon season (July–September) and peak sales in Q3 (festive push) and Q4 (year-end construction push).
3. **Invoice Numbering**: Unique sequential numbering in the format `SLS/2024-25/XXXX` (where `XXXX` is a 4-digit zero-padded sequence).
4. **Line Quantities**: Retail/dealer batch quantities. Randomly sample quantities from 10 to 150 pieces per line item depending on customer type:
   - `Distributor` purchases: 80–150 pieces per line.
   - `Retailer` purchases: 20–80 pieces per line.
   - `Contractor` purchases: 40–120 pieces per line.
   - `Fabricator` purchases: 10–30 pieces per line.
5. **Customer Selection Skew**:
   - `Retailer` customers: 50% of total invoices.
   - `Fabricator` customers: 20% of total invoices.
   - `Contractor` customers: 15% of total invoices.
   - `Distributor` customers: 15% of total invoices.
6. **Negotiation Variance by Customer Tier**:
   - `Distributor` sales: Sample variance uniformly between -4.00% and -2.00% (volume discounts).
   - `Contractor` sales: Sample variance between -2.50% and 0.00% (project pricing).
   - `Retailer` sales: Sample variance between -1.50% and +1.00% (standard dealer price).
   - `Fabricator` sales: Sample variance between 0.00% and +3.00% (spot cash markup).
7. **Initial Payment Status**: Generate all records with `payment_status = 'Unpaid'` and outstanding value equal to the full invoice value. (Settlements are simulated during Cashbook generation).
8. **Onboarding Constraint**: Customer selection is constrained to customers with an `onboarding_date` prior to the `sales_date`.
